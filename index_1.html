📖 How to Play Street Life

🎮 MOVEMENT

WASD or Arrow Keys to walk. Click anywhere to walk there. Click buildings or people to interact. Press P to open your phone.

💼 CAREERS (6 paths)

Walk to a job site and start a career. On-the-Job: starts Level 1, 5 shifts per level. College Cert ($150): starts Level 3, 10 shifts per level. Both reach Level 8. You can only have ONE career at a time.

🎮 STREAMING & CONTENT

Buy an online course at HOME ($80) or get certified at College ($100). Stream from home — pick how many hours each session. More hours = more followers + money but more energy drain. Get a camera ($200) to unlock Content Creator. These count as your career slot.

🚗 GIG WORK

Open your Phone (📱) and go to Apps tab. Sign up for Delivery, TaskRabbit, or Busking. After signing up, you'll receive task requests on your Messages tab. Accept or Decline each one. Each task pays differently.

💊 DRUG GAME

Plant weed seeds at home ($10 each). Water them to grow faster. 5 stages from seedling to harvest. Cook drugs at Big Mike's or Warehouse. Toggle dealer mode ON your phone to get deal requests. Sell to Corner Store = less $ but zero heat. Street deals = more $ but heat risk. You can stay completely small and just sell to the store.

📱 PHONE

Bottom left button or press P. Messages: accept/decline tasks and deal requests. Apps: sign up for gig apps. Stream: check follower stats. Dealer: toggle dealer mode, manage stash.

💵 MONEY & RENT

Rent is $25/day auto-deducted at midnight. Very easy to cover with one shift or gig. Watch your stats panel for upcoming costs.
