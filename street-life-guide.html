Getting Started
HOW TO PLAY
1
👤

Create Your Character

Pick your name, gender, skin tone, hair color and background. Your background affects your starting money and skills.

2
🗺️

Explore the City

Tap or click buildings to interact. Walk up to people to talk. The city has two sides — rough and nice — each with different opportunities.

3
💵

Make Money

Get a career, pick up gig work, start streaming, or run the streets. Rent is $25/day so keep your cash flowing.

4
👑

Build Your Life

Level up your career, save money, build credit, buy your apartment and unlock the Made milestone.

Controls

Character Creation
BACKGROUNDS
😤
Broke Hustler
Start with $50 and a boosted Hustle skill. Hardest start but you know how to grind. Best for players who want the full struggle experience.
👷
Hard Worker
Start with $200 and a head start on career readiness. Easier early game. Good for players new to the game who want to focus on careers.
😎
Smooth Talker
Start with $100 and boosted Charisma. Better at building relationships, faster promotions, better deals. Great for the social playstyle.
Work
CAREERS

Every career has 8 levels. Start on-the-job at Level 1 (5 shifts per level) OR get a college certificate for $150 to start at Level 3 (10 shifts per level). Both paths reach Level 8. You can only have ONE career at a time.

🩺
Medical
Hospital · Orderly → Doctor
Start as an Orderly and work your way up to Doctor. Highest paying career at max level. Dr. Chen gives you discounts if you build a relationship with her.
💻
Tech
Tech Hub · Help Desk → CTO
Start at the Help Desk and grind to CTO. Study tech at the Tech Hub to level up your skill faster. Strong mid-game income.
👨‍🍳
Culinary
City Diner · Dishwasher → Owner
Work your way from Dishwasher to Restaurant Owner. Chef Rosa at the diner is your connection. Good for players who like cooking drugs too.
⚖️
Law
Law Office · Paralegal → Sr. Partner
Highest paying career of all at max level. A law career also helps you win more money if you ever need to file a lawsuit. Attorney James is your contact.
🔧
Mechanic
Auto Shop · Lot Attendant → Shop Owner
Start at the Auto Shop on the rough side. Steady income, great for players who want to stay on the rough side of town.
📈
Business
Business Park · Clerk → CEO
Start as a Clerk at the Business Park on the nice side. Strong all-around career. CEO pays $320 per shift at max level.
Side Hustle
GIG WORK & STREAMING
🚗
Gig Work
Open your Phone → Apps tab to sign up for gig apps. Three options:

🚗 Delivery Quick tasks, steady pay
🔨 TaskRabbit Odd jobs, varies each time
🎵 Busking Street performance, tip-based

Accept or decline tasks from your Messages tab. No career required.
🎮
Streaming
Buy an online course at home ($80) or get certified at College ($100). Then stream from your apartment.

Build followers for passive daily income. Get a camera ($200) to unlock Content Creator for higher earnings. More hours = more followers but more energy drain.

At 1,000+ followers you are monetized!
The Streets
THE DRUG GAME

This path is high risk, high reward. You can make a lot of money fast — but police watch, heat builds, and Big Mike always wants his cut.

🌱
Grow Operation
Plant weed seeds at home ($10 each). Water them to grow faster. 5 growth stages from seedling to harvest. Each plant gives you 4 weed when ready.

Tip: Check your Inventory panel to see plant progress.
⚗️
Cooking
Cook at Big Mikes or the Warehouse.

2 Weed → 1 Pill
3 Pills → 1 Powder (needs Cook Lv2)

Powder is the most valuable product. Build your Cook skill to unlock it.
💊
Selling
Two ways to sell:

🏪 Corner Store Safe, no heat, 60% value
📱 Street Deals Full price but heat risk

Enable Dealer Mode on your phone to receive deal requests. High heat brings police. Stay below 50% heat to stay safe.
🚔
Police & Heat
Heat builds from street dealing. Watch the wanted stars.

3+ Stars Police car spawns and chases you
Caught Drugs confiscated + $150 fine

Lay low at home to reduce heat. Get legal help at the Law Office ($200 drops heat 30%).
Finance
BANKING & CREDIT

Visit the City Bank on the nice side of town. Tap the CREDIT score in the top right corner anytime to open the bank.

💰
Savings Account
Deposit cash to earn 2% interest every week. Money in the bank grows automatically. Use "Deposit All" to move everything but $25 to savings at once.
🏦
Loans
Borrow money based on your credit score. Better score = bigger loans and lower interest rates. Pay on time to build credit. Miss payments and your score drops + a 5% penalty is added to the balance.
📊
Credit Score
Score ranges from 300 to 850.

300-499 Very Poor — no loans
500-669 Fair/Poor — small loans
670-849 Good — buy apartment!
850 Excellent — best rates

Pay rent on time, hold a job, and save money to boost your score.
🏠
Buy Your Apartment
Get your credit score to 670+ and save $5,000. Buy your apartment and never pay rent again. This is one of the biggest milestones in the game.
Story
THE BIG MIKE DEBT

Every game starts with a $500 debt to Big Mike. He fronted you money to get into the city. What happens next depends on you.

⚠️ THE TIMELINE

DAY 1
Big Mike texts you. You owe $500. 30 days to pay. Pay him anytime by visiting his building.
DAY 7
First warning text. He is watching. You still have time.
DAY 21
Final warning. His patience is running out.
DAY 30
Deadline missed. Big Mike cuts you off. No supply for 7 days. Relationship damaged.
DAY 37
He sends someone. Screen goes red. You get jumped. Health drops to critical. Cash taken. Debt grows 50%.
✅
Pay Early
Pay before Day 30 — Big Mike respects you. Relationship +25. Credit boost. Better drug prices unlocked.
⚖️
After the Beating
Attorney James texts you. Go to the Law Office to file a lawsuit. Win $800-$1,200 if your heat is below 50.
Goals
MILESTONES

🌱
Survivor
Make it to Day 7 without getting evicted. The hardest days are the first ones.
💼
Employed
Reach Level 3 in any career. You have a real job now. Things are looking up.
🏠
Settled
Own your apartment. No more rent. You built something that is yours.
💰
Stacking
Save $10,000 in the bank. Real money. Real options. The city opens up.
👑
Made
Level 8 career + own your home + $25,000 saved. You came from nothing and built everything.
Pro Tips
SURVIVAL GUIDE
💤 Sleep is Important
Low energy means slower progress. Sleep 8 hours at home regularly. Burnout above 80% blocks career shifts.
🍔 Keep Hunger Up
Below 15% hunger your health starts dropping. Eat at the Corner Store ($10) or cook at home ($5).
💾 Save Often
The game auto-saves every 60 seconds but tap the Save button in the pause menu after big moments.
🏦 Deposit Cash
Cash in your pocket does not earn interest. Move it to savings at the City Bank to make money while you sleep.
🌡️ Watch Your Heat
Keep heat below 50% if you are running drugs. Above 50 brings police. Above 70 they actively chase you.
👥 Build Relationships
Talk to Dr. Chen for medical discounts. Talk to Attorney James for lawsuit bonuses. Relationships matter.
🎓 College is Worth It
$150 for a certificate lets you start a career at Level 3 instead of Level 1. Pays for itself in 2 shifts.
💊 Mix Income Streams
Career + gig work + streaming passive income = fastest way to stack money. Do not rely on one source.
